  <div class="me">
    <div>
      <p>Hi, I am <b>Anthony Tong</b>, a third-year undergraduate double majoring in
        Computer Science and Applied Mathematics at UC San Diego, broadly interested in
        building tools for interdisciplinary AI applications.</p>
      <p>I currently work with Professor <a href="https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~gary/">Garrison W. Cottrell</a>
        on the SMART (Small Molecule Accurate Recognition Technology) project. I was previously
        advised by Professor <a href="https://edwinsolares.com/">Edwin A. Solares</a> on
        computer-vision projects in species rescue and agricultural and language conservation,
        and by Professors <a href="https://www.unlv.edu/people/shengjie-zhai">Shengjie Zhai</a>
        and <a href="https://www.unlv.edu/people/ming-zhu-phd">Ming Zhu</a> on LLM tokenization
        and domain adaptation. I am also grateful to have worked with Professor
        <a href="https://mazumdar.ucsd.edu/">Arya Mazumdar</a> through the Regents Scholars
        Research Initiative on logistic regression and diffusion generative models.</p>
