Third-year undergraduate · Regents' Scholar · SMART Lab

Hi, I am Anthony Tong — a third-year undergraduate double majoring in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics at UC San Diego, broadly interested in building tools for interdisciplinary AI applications.

I currently work with Professor Garrison W. Cottrell on the SMART (Small Molecule Accurate Recognition Technology) project. I was previously advised by Professor Edwin A. Solares on computer-vision projects in species rescue and agricultural and language conservation, and by Professors Shengjie Zhai and Ming Zhu on LLM tokenization and domain adaptation. I'm also grateful to have worked with Professor Arya Mazumdar through the Regents Scholars Research Initiative on logistic regression and diffusion generative models.
